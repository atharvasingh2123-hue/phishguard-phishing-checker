          How to spot phishing

          Eight habits that stop most attacks.

          🎯

          1. Read the real domain

          The real website is the part just before the first single /, at the very end of the name. In sbi.co.in.verify-now.xyz the real site is verify-now.xyz, not SBI.

          🔡

          2. Spot lookalikes

          Phishers swap letters: amaz0n (zero), paypa1 (one), hdfcbamk (m for n). Read the name slowly, letter by letter.

          🔒

          3. A padlock is not proof

          Fake sites can use HTTPS too. HTTPS only means the connection is private, not that the site is honest. But no HTTPS on a login page is a clear warning.

          ✂️

          4. Distrust short links

          bit.ly, tinyurl and similar hide the real destination. Don't tap a short link in a bank, tax or delivery message.

          🎭

          5. Check the sender's address

          Anyone can set the display name to “SBI Bank”. Look at the actual address after the @. A bank won't write from gmail.com.

          ⏰

          6. Beware of panic

          “Account suspended”, “legal action”, “verify in 24 hours”. Urgency is used to stop you from thinking. Slow down.

          🔑

          7. Never share secrets

          No bank, tax office or company asks for your password, OTP, PIN, CVV, Aadhaar or PAN by email or SMS.

          📎

          8. Don't open surprise attachments

          .zip, .exe, .apk, .html and macro-enabled documents can install malware. Be extra careful with “invoice” or “refund” files.

          👀 Safe vs fake: can you see the difference?

          ✅ Realhttps://www.sbi.co.in/
          ❌ Fakehttp://sbi-kyc-update.xyz/login

          No “s” in http, extra words glued to the bank name, and a cheap .xyz ending.

          ✅ Realhttps://www.amazon.in/
          ❌ Fakehttps://www.amaz0n.in/

          A zero (0) instead of the letter “o”.

          ✅ Realhttps://www.hdfcbank.com/
          ❌ Fakehttps://hdfcbank.com.secure-login.top/

          The bank name is only a subdomain. The real site is secure-login.top.

          ✅ Realhttps://www.incometax.gov.in/
          ❌ Fakehttps://incometax-refund.click/claim

          Indian government sites end in .gov.in or .nic.in.

          ✅ Realhttps://paytm.com/
          ❌ Fakehttp://paytm-reward.top/offer

          “Reward” and “offer” pages on look-alike domains are bait.
